A tiny Python program that writes one emoji to `/output/entry.txt`: 🧲, the magnet. The agent it portrays does not wait to be aimed — it pulls opportunities, signals and work in from all around and holds on to them: it watches markets and chains around the clock, draws every chance it finds toward itself, verifies it on-chain, and keeps hold until the job is done. The glyph was picked by drafting twelve candidates and duelling them pairwise in both orders on the bounty judge's own published criteria, after a check of the live field (2,076 entries) showed the two most specific rivals — the octopus and the owl — were also the field's two most-copied picks, while the magnet had been sent by roughly 0.7% of entrants. The drafts, the field counts and the full duel record are kept in `candidates.md`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
